The Election Process

A step-by-step guide to participating in democracy

1. Registration

Ensure you meet the eligibility criteria and register to vote before your local deadline.

  • Citizen of the country
  • 18+ years old
  • Valid ID required

2. Research

Learn about the candidates, their platforms, and the issues on the ballot in your district.

  • Read unbiased sources
  • Review voting records
  • Understand propositions

3. Plan

Decide how and when you will vote. Will you vote by mail, early in person, or on Election Day?

  • Find polling location
  • Check voting hours
  • Request absentee ballot

4. Vote

Cast your ballot! Follow the instructions carefully and make your voice heard.

  • Bring required ID
  • Follow polling rules
  • Verify your choices

Election Timeline

Key dates you need to know for the upcoming election cycle

January - June

Primary Elections

Voters choose which candidates will represent their political party in the general election.

July - August

National Conventions

Political parties finalize their choice for presidential and vice-presidential nominees.

September - October

Debates & Campaigns

Candidates participate in public debates and intensify their campaign efforts nationwide.

First Tuesday of November

Election Day

Voters cast their final ballots to determine the winners of the various offices.
